note: The second part of the journey north: islands, the midnight sun, a Sunday in a northern church. Descriptive past tense, comparisons and connectors (dessuten, likevel, derfor). The facts about the midnight sun and the places are general and unchecked; a Norwegian reviewer should check them. No real church is named.
## Nordover
Etter Trondheim gikk skipet langs kysten mot nord. Dagene ble lysere, og natten ble lys. {sam} var på dekk hver kveld, for han ville se alt. || After Trondheim the ship went along the coast towards the north. The days got lighter, and the night became light. Sam was on deck every evening, because he wanted to see everything.
[nico] Blir det mørkt noen gang? Jeg vil se stjernene! || Does it ever get dark? I want to see the stars!
[ana] Ikke nå. I sommer er det lyst hele natten her i nord. Det er uvanlig for oss som bor i sør, men folk her kjenner det godt. || Not now. In summer it is light all night here in the north. It is unusual for us who live in the south, but people here know it well.

## Øyene
Neste dag så de mange øyer. De lå i havet, og bak dem var fjellene. Det lå snø på fjellene, selv om det var sommer. || The next day they saw many islands. They lay in the sea, and behind them were the mountains. There was snow on the mountains, even though it was summer.
{sam} var på dekk og så på øyene. Det var vakrere enn noe han hadde sett i bøker. Dessuten var det rolig, og bare vinden og bølgene var å høre. || Sam was on deck and looked at the islands. It was more beautiful than anything he had seen in books. Besides, it was calm, and only the wind and the waves were to be heard.
I en liten havn var det små hus og fisk ute i vinden. Det var tørrfisk, som {nico} hadde skrevet om på skolen. || In a little harbour there were small houses and fish out in the wind. It was dried fish, which Nils had written about at school.
[nico] Se! Det er tørrfisk! Fiskerne drar ut på havet og fanger fisk, og så blir fisken tørr her! Jeg skrev om det i prosjektet mitt! || Look! It is dried fish! The fishermen go out onto the sea and catch fish, and then the fish becomes dry here! I wrote about it in my project!
Alle lo, men {luis} var stolt. Dessuten var det han som hadde lært {nico} å skrive. || Everyone laughed, but Lars was proud. Besides, it was he who had taught Nils to write.

## Midnattssol
I Tromsø var sola ute hele natten. Klokka tolv var himmelen lys, og klokka to var den like lys. Midnattssolen var uvanlig for {sam}, og ingen kunne sove. Dessuten var himmelen så vakker at han ikke ville sove. || In Tromsø the sun was out all night. At twelve o'clock the sky was bright, and at two o'clock it was just as bright. The midnight sun was unusual for Sam, and nobody could sleep. Besides, the sky was so beautiful that he did not want to sleep.
[ana] Her er forskjellen mellom sommer og vinter stor. Om vinteren er det mørkt nesten hele dagen. Nå er det lyst hele natten. || Here the difference between summer and winter is big. In winter it is dark almost the whole day. Now it is light all night.
[sam] Hva er en midnattssol, egentlig? Det er ikke uvanlig for dere, men for meg er det som en drøm. Jeg tror ikke at det er noen forskjell mellom natt og dag nå. || What is a midnight sun, actually? It is not unusual for you, but for me it is like a dream. I do not think that there is any difference between night and day now.
De vandret i fjellet om natten, for ingen ville sove. Det var kaldt, men sola var over havet, og himmelen var lys. {sam} tenkte at han aldri hadde sett noe så vakkert. || They hiked in the mountains at night, because nobody wanted to sleep. It was cold, but the sun was over the sea, and the sky was bright. Sam thought that he had never seen anything so beautiful.
[luis] Derfor sover vi dårlig, men vi vandrer mye! Det er stor forskjell på nord og sør, og landet er vakkert i nord og i sør. Jeg liker å vandre når det er lyst. || Therefore we sleep badly, but we hike a lot! There is a big difference between north and south, and the country is beautiful in the north and in the south. I like hiking when it is light.
[nico] Midnattssolen er best! Himmelen er lys som om dagen! Jeg skal skrive et nytt prosjekt om den! || The midnight sun is best! The sky is light as in the daytime! I will write a new project about it!

## Søndag
På søndag gikk de i kirka i Tromsø. Den var liten, men det var mange folk der. Presten snakket rolig, og {sam} forstod nesten alt. || On Sunday they went to the church in Tromsø. It was small, but there were many people there. The pastor spoke calmly, and Sam understood almost everything.
Etter gudstjenesten sa en gammel dame til ham: «Du snakker godt norsk. Hvor lenge har du bodd her?» || After the service an old lady said to him: "You speak good Norwegian. How long have you lived here?"
[sam] I snart to år. Jeg hadde ikke trodd at jeg skulle forstå presten på norsk. || For almost two years. I had not thought that I would understand the pastor in Norwegian.
Den kvelden ba {sam} på dekk, mens sola fortsatt var ute: «Takk, Gud, for øyene, for fjellene og for alle som har reist med meg. Takk for at jeg forstår mer enn før.» || That evening Sam prayed on deck, while the sun was still out: "Thank you, God, for the islands, for the mountains and for all who have travelled with me. Thank you that I understand more than before."

I lugaren leste {luis} en krim, og {nico} spurte om noen måtte dø i boka. {luis} og {sam} lo begge to. {nico} hadde pyntet lugaren med små stjerner av papir, som på treet hjemme, og han hadde med seg en mandel i veska. Det var hans egen tradisjon, og han sov likevel ikke. Sola var der hele tiden. {sam} tenkte på hverdagen hjemme og på løypa i fjellet der han hadde gått på ski. || In the cabin Lars read a crime novel, and Nils asked whether someone had to die in the book. Lars and Sam both laughed. Nils had decorated the cabin with little stars of paper, like on the tree at home, and he had an almond in his bag. It was his own tradition, and he did not sleep all the same. The sun was there all the time. Sam thought of everyday life at home and of the trail in the mountains where he had gone skiing.
Blant folk om bord var det mange som sov dårlig. Faktisk var det kapteinen som ga dem et tips: Gå en tur på dekk! Prisen for en tur på dekk var ingenting. || Among the passengers there were many who slept badly. Actually it was the captain who gave them a tip: Go for a walk on deck! The price of a walk on deck was nothing.
{nico} slo av lyset i lugaren, men det var like lyst. || Nils switched off the light in the cabin, but it was just as bright.
