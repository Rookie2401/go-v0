# Modern standard Albanian open corpus

Checked 2026-10-02. This bounded corpus contains 23 coherent article or article-section documents (8,863 source words) from 9 pages on the Albanian Wikipedia. The material is modern standard Albanian as written by the source pages; no translation or generated prose was used. Difficulty is an editorial estimate only, and there is no native-speaker or A0 review yet. The `Familja` page and `Shqipëria — Kultura` section are retained in the raw cache but quarantined from the manifest because their orthography fails the modern-standard quality bar.

The acquisition helper is [scripts/acquire-sq-modern-open.mts](../../scripts/acquire-sq-modern-open.mts). It reads Wikimedia's official rendered-page endpoint, `https://api.wikimedia.org/core/v1/wikipedia/sq/page/{title}/html`, and stores the exact retrieved page in [data/sources/sq-modern-open/pages](../../data/sources/sq-modern-open/pages). Each page keeps its revision ID, modification timestamp, API URL, source SHA-256, and the exact Wikipedia page URL in [source-manifest.json](../../data/sources/sq-modern-open/source-manifest.json). Run it again with `npx vite-node scripts/acquire-sq-modern-open.mts --refresh --date=YYYY-MM-DD` to retrieve current revisions, or without `--refresh` to rebuild from the cached pages.

The selected units cover food and daily life (`Ushqimi`), animals and nature (`Qeni`, `Macja`, `Bleta`, `Mali`, `Deti`), and ordinary geography, climate, transport, demography, economy, culture, and history sections from Tirana, Albania, and Durrës. Section boundaries use the exact Albanian headings in the rendered source. Navigation, references, related-links sections, and Parsoid infobox metadata are left out. Inline links and emphasis are removed while the source paragraph wording is retained.

The actual mix is 17 place/geography units, 5 society units, 4 geography units, 3 animal units, 3 nature units, 3 weather units, 3 history units, 3 daily-life units, 2 work/economy units, and one each for food, travel and culture (some units carry more than one topic). The shortest units are about 30 words and the longest are about 1,300 words. The collection contains scientific vocabulary in the animal articles and food-additive, allergy and other health statements in `Ushqimi`; these are source content, not advice, and remain queued for native review. The collection is an intermediate bridge estimate only and carries no CEFR or A0 claim.

The documents are in [documents.json](../../data/sources/sq-modern-open/documents.json), and [library-manifest.json](../../data/sources/sq-modern-open/library-manifest.json) binds that exact file hash to the `sq-modern-open` lazy-library work at Albanian stage 7, under the existing `library` stream. The companion work record is [modernAlbanian.ts](../../src/course/modernAlbanian.ts), ready for the course catalogue to import. Every document records its exact revision URL, the global CC BY-SA 4.0 licence, attribution, source-fidelity provenance, a content signature, and a named machine/source-fidelity review. `needs-native-review` remains explicit. The review records source fidelity and licensing metadata; it does not claim native linguistic review or certify the material for beginners.

Wikimedia's local licence record is [Wikipedia:CC BY-SA](https://sq.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:CC_BY-SA), and the licence text is [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). The corpus keeps the source attribution and share-alike requirement with every document. It is not a Bible substitute: the modern standard Albanian Bible is linked through the exact [Së bashku 2020 page on YouVersion](https://www.bible.com/versions/3631), with the app's Bible access and rights record documented in [BIBLE_ACCESS.md](../BIBLE_ACCESS.md).
