=== la-a1-1-05 | letter | correspondence | A letter to my mother | Epistula Samuelis | letter,family,town,friends
## Epistula
Samuel in domo est. Samuel in mensa epistulam scribit. Mater longe est. || Samuel is in the house. Samuel writes a letter at the table. His mother is far away.

## Samuel matri salutem dicit
Mater cara, salve! || Dear mother, hello!
Ego bene valeo. In urbe nova sum. Urbs bona est. || I am well. I am in a new town. The town is good.
Domus mea parva est, sed bona est. In domo mensam habeo. Fenestram quoque habeo. || My house is small, but it is good. In the house I have a table. I have a window too.
Hic amicos habeo. Anna amica mea est, et in valetudinario laborat. Lucius magister est, et in schola laborat. Martha panem et lac vendit. Marcus puer bonus est. || Here I have friends. Anna is my friend. She works at the clinic. Lucius is a teacher, and he works at school. Martha sells bread and milk. Marcus is a good boy.
Quomodo vales, mater cara? Et pater, quomodo valet? Ubi est frater meus? Ubi est soror mea? || How are you, dear mother? And father, how is he? Where is my brother? Where is my sister?
Cras ad ecclesiam eo, et gratias Deo ago. Hodie panem et lac habeo. Bene est. || Tomorrow I go to church, and I give thanks to God. Today I have bread and milk. It is well.
Scribe mihi, mater! || Write to me, mother!
Vale! Pax tibi, mater. Pax tibi, pater. || Goodbye! Peace to you, mother. Peace to you, father.
Samuel tuus || Your Samuel

## Epistula in foro
Samuel in foro est. Martha Samuelem videt. || Samuel is in the market. Martha sees Samuel.

[marta] Salve, Samuel! Quid est hoc? Estne epistula? || Hello, Samuel! What is this? Is it a letter?
[sam] Epistula est. Matri meae scribo. Hodie scribo. || It is a letter. I am writing to my mother. Today I write.
[marta] Bene! Urbs nova bona est, et hic amicos habes. || Good! The new town is good, and here you have friends.
[sam] Amicos bonos habeo, et gratias Deo ago. || I have good friends, and I give thanks to God.
[marta] Scribe, Samuel. Mater cara epistulam amat. Pater quoque bonus est. || Write, Samuel. Your dear mother loves a letter. Your father is good too.
[sam] Bene! Gratias tibi ago, Martha. Panem et lac emo. || Good! Thank you, Martha. I buy bread and milk.
[marta] Vale, Samuel! Pax tibi. Ecce panis, ecce lac. Panem et lac tibi do. || Goodbye, Samuel! Peace to you. Look, bread, look, milk. I give you bread and milk.
[sam] Gratias tibi ago! Bene valeo, et fessus non sum. || Thank you! I am well, and I am not tired.
[ana] Salvete! Quomodo valetis? Ubi est Lucius? || Hello! How are you? Where is Lucius?
[marta] Bene valemus, Anna. Lucius in schola est. || We are well, Anna. Lucius is at school.
[ana] Bene! Panem et lac emo. Gratias tibi ago, Martha. Vale, Samuel! || Good! I buy bread and milk. Thank you, Martha. Goodbye, Samuel!
