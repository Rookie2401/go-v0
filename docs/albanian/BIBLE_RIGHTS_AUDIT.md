# Albanian Bible rights audit

Checked 2026-10-02. No authorized redistributable full standard-Albanian edition was found.

eBible.org's [translation catalogue](https://ebible.org/Scriptures/) lists an Albanian-territory Aromanian translation (`rup`) with an open licence. Aromanian is a different language and cannot substitute for standard Albanian. The [Albanian Bible Society](https://albania.ibep-se.com/home/) identifies the complete 2020 “Së Bashku” edition and its publisher. [YouVersion's edition page](https://www.bible.com/versions/3631) credits © Interconfessional Bible Society of Albania but does not grant permission to bundle or redistribute the full text.

Public-domain labels on [BibleGateway's ALB page](https://www.biblegateway.com/versions/Albanian-Bible-ALB/) explicitly say that publication history and further copyright data are unknown. That is useful discovery evidence, but not primary authorization for app distribution. GoLearn therefore keeps Albanian online-only and does not copy, infer, or fabricate Scripture wording.

The concrete acquisition path is written permission or an explicit open licence from the Interconfessional Bible Society of Albania for the exact edition and full-text redistribution, together with a downloadable source carrying verse boundaries (USFM, USX, or equivalent). Once those are supplied, the existing Scripture importer and canon checks can build the edition.
