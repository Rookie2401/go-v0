# Contextual syntax design (Linguistic Engine V2, Stage 4)

Shallow dependency relations between the words of one sentence, from each adapter's rules over the readings its `analyze`
gave. No parser model is trained or downloaded; no sentence leaves the device. The per-language rules, gold sets and
measured scores are in `SYNTAX-sq.md`, `SYNTAX-es.md`, `SYNTAX-de.md`, `SYNTAX-hi.md`, `SYNTAX-th.md`, `SYNTAX-nb-nn.md`
(written by the language lanes; a language whose file is missing has no rules yet and the card says so).

## The contract: `src/core/syntax.ts`

- `SyntaxRelation { dependent, head?, relation, label, basis[], confidence, alternatives?, status }` — `relation` is a
  Universal Dependencies label where one fits (`nsubj`, `obj`, `iobj`, `obl`, `nmod`, `amod`, `det`, `case`, `aux`, `cop`,
  `mark`, `advmod`, `advcl`, `acl`, `ccomp`, `xcomp`, `conj`, `cc`, `nummod`, `appos`, `vocative`, `discourse`, `expl`,
  `compound`, `flat`, `fixed`, `clf`, `punct`, `dep`), `label` is the relation in plain words for a learner ("subject of
  'lexon'"), `basis` is why the rule attached it there (never empty), `alternatives` are the readings the rules could not
  rule out (`head`, `relation`, `confidence`, `why`), `status` is `generated` or `conflicted`.
- `SyntaxAnalysis { languageId, analyzer, tokens, relations, clauses?, notes, attached }` — `attached` is the share of word
  tokens that got a relation; `clauses` are `ClauseSpan { start, end, kind, marker?, label }`.
- `relationOf(a, i)`, `dependentsOf(a, i)`, `RELATION_WORDS` (plain-words names for the labels).
- Adapter method: `syntax?(tokens: RawToken[], analyses: AnalyzedToken[]): SyntaxAnalysis | undefined`.

## Honesty rules

1. A relation is `generated` by rules; nothing marks it verified. The card prints "Generated by the analyser's rules
   (adapter-xx@n); N % of this sentence's words were attached. Not reviewed by a person."
2. Where agreement or order cannot decide (nominative = accusative syncretism, two nouns around the verb, `se` passive vs
   reflexive, a Thai stative verb as modifier vs predicate), the rule keeps both readings and says why; the card shows the
   chosen one with `‼ not decided` and the others under "Also possible".
3. A word the rules cannot attach gets no relation; the card says "the analyser has syntax rules but could not attach this
   word" instead of guessing `dep`.
4. The rules never change `analyze`'s readings or an adapter's version.

## On the card (`packages/golearn-extension/src/parser/index.ts: syntaxNoteFor`, `popup/card.ts: fillSyntax`)

For a single selected word with sentence context the parser calls `adapter.syntax` once and keeps, as `CardModel.syntax`:
the relation, its label and head word, the basis lines, the alternatives with their reasons, the dependents in sentence
order, the clause the word sits in, the analysis notes and the attached share. The Phrase & syntax panel shows them in that
order. Without rules the old `role` line stays (`role: not available from this analyser`); the role line falls back to the
relation's label when the reading has no role of its own. Tests: `test/extension/syntax.card.test.ts`.

## Evaluation

Each lane writes `data/gold/<lang>/syntax-dev.json` (≥ 40 sentences from the owner's course texts, heads and UD relations
labelled by the lane: AI-judged, unreviewed, no native speaker) and a probe `scripts/probe-syntax-<lang>.mts` reporting the
attached share, the relation distribution, the share of relations with alternatives, UAS/LAS on the gold file and the
milliseconds per sentence; where a UD treebank is on disk the lane scores against it too. The measured numbers are in the
per-language SYNTAX docs and in GOLD-REPORT-2026-10-10.md; a test floor is a number measured on 2026-10-10, not a target.

## Not modelled (all languages)

Long-distance dependencies beyond one clause, coordination of clauses with shared arguments, ellipsis, non-projective
word order, discourse-level reference; sentences the tokenizer did not split correctly. The notes of each analysis name the
sentence-level gaps the rules noticed.
