   notes: Thai words never change form: there is no conjugation, declension, gender or number marking, so there is no table, and what a word does is shown by the words around it. || Tense, aspect and negation are separate words placed beside the verb (กำลัง, จะ, เคย, แล้ว, อยู่, ไม่), never changes to the verb itself. || Verbs are often strung together with no linking word (serial verbs, ไปซื้อ "go and buy"); the card shows each verb as its own word.
