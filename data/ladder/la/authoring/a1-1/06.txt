## Dies Dominica
Hodie dies Dominica est. Dies Dominica dies Dei est. Omnes ad ecclesiam eunt. || Today is Sunday. Sunday is God's day. All go to church.
Martha ad ecclesiam it. Anna et Lucius ad ecclesiam eunt. Marcus et Samuel quoque ad ecclesiam eunt. || Martha goes to church. Anna and Lucius go to church. Marcus and Samuel go to church too.
Ecclesia non longe est. Ecclesia magna est, et multi homines in ecclesia sunt. || The church is not far. The church is big, and many people are in the church.

## In ecclesia
In ecclesia homines Deum laudant. Homines Deum amant. Homines Deo gratias agunt. || In church people praise God. People love God. People give thanks to God.
Homines audiunt, et orant, et cantant. || People listen, and pray, and sing.
Sacerdos quoque in ecclesia est. Sacerdos Deum laudat. Sacerdos dicit: Pax vobis! || The priest is in the church too. The priest praises God. The priest says: Peace to you!
Omnes dicunt: Deo gratias! || All say: Thanks be to God!
Post ecclesiam Martha in foro est. Martha panem vendit. || After church Martha is in the market. Martha sells bread.

## Samuel et Martha
Samuel ad ecclesiam venit. Marthae dicit: Salve! || Samuel comes to the church. He says to Martha: Hello!

[sam] Salve, Martha! Hodie ad ecclesiam venio. || Hello, Martha! Today I come to church.
[marta] Bene! Hodie bonum est. || Good! It is good today.
[sam] Ubi est Anna? Estne in ecclesia? || Where is Anna? Is she in church?
[marta] Anna ibi est! Et Lucius ibi est. || Anna is there! And Lucius is there too.
[sam] Et Marcus? Ubi est puer? || And Marcus? Where is the boy?
[marta] Marcus hic est. Ecce Marcus! Ibi cantat. || Marcus is here. Look, Marcus! He is singing there.
[nico] Ego Deum laudo! Libenter in ecclesia sum. || I praise God! I am gladly in church.
[sam] Et ego Deum laudo. Gratias Deo ago. || And I praise God. I give thanks to God.
[sam] Martha, tu quoque oras? || Martha, do you pray too?
[marta] Oro, et Deo gratias ago. Libenter oro, libenter canto. || I pray, and I give thanks to God. I gladly pray, I gladly sing.

Lucius et Anna in ecclesia sunt. Lucius Samuelem videt. || Lucius and Anna are in the church. Lucius sees Samuel.

[luis] Salve, Samuel! Quomodo vales? || Hello, Samuel! How are you?
[sam] Bene valeo, Luci. Et tu? Quomodo vales? || I am well, Lucius. And you? How are you?
[luis] Bene valeo, sed fessus sum. Anna quoque fessa est. || I am well, but I am tired. Anna is tired too.
[ana] Hodie in valetudinario non laboro. Hic sum, et bene est. || Today I do not work at the clinic. I am here, and it is well.

Sacerdos dicit: Pax vobis! Omnes dicunt: Deo gratias! || The priest says: Peace to you! All say: Thanks be to God!
Post ecclesiam Samuel in domo Marthae est. Panem edunt et lac bibunt. || After church Samuel is in Martha's house. They eat bread and drink milk.
Martha Deum amat, et omnes homines Deum amant. || Martha loves God, and all people love God.
