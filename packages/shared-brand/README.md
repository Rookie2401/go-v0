# Go identity
The shared Go logo uses a bold G and a globe as the O, based on the supplied Global Outreach photo, in the Western Canon brown.
Colour, decided by the owner on 2026-10-27: the mark is the Western Canon BROWN (#6b4f2a) on paper (#f7f2e8), the reading palette of both apps, not the navy it was first generated in. The two masters below were recoloured from the navy originals (every pixel's ink amount re-expressed as brown; the transparent master keeps its alpha). The headers (GoReview, the GoLearn setup page, and since 2026-10-28 the GoLearn action popup, which reads its copy from packages/golearn-extension/icons/go-logo.png; the setup page reads its own copy, packages/golearn-extension/app/icons/go-logo.png, by a URL resolved against the page, because its extension build is a classic script with no import.meta.url, 2026-10-28) draw go-logo.png as a CSS mask filled with the accent colour, so the mark is brown on paper and the lighter brown of the dark theme. Both GoLearn and GoReview use this mark; their app names stay visible alongside it.
- go-logo.png: transparent master for application headers.
- go-icon-source.png: opaque square master for app icons.
- go-globe-rim.png and go-globe-frame.png: the still parts of GoReview’s loading mark (packages/goreview/app/loading.ts): the rim’s inner ring, and the rim’s outer ring with the meridian and stand. go-globe-land.png: the logo’s continents, which the app no longer shows flat; it is the source of the sheet below. All three are cut from go-logo.png by scripts/split-go-globe.cjs (which also writes go-globe-geometry.json, read for the clip circle in the stylesheet; 2026-10-28).
- go-globe-turn.png: the land of the loading mark, one whole turn on a real sphere, 120 frames in 12 columns and 10 rows, rendered by scripts/render-go-globe-turn.cjs (2026-11-03). The logo’s Americas, Greenland and Iceland are its own art, unprojected; the rest of the world is drawn in that script in coarse coasts rounded to the logo’s style, an emblem and not a map. Run both scripts, split first, after the logo changes.
