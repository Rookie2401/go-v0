## Iter
Hodie dies bonus est. Anna et Samuel in via ambulant. || Today is a good day. Anna and Samuel walk on the road.
Anna fessa non est. Hodie in valetudinario non laborat. Samuel quoque fessus non est. || Anna is not tired. Today she does not work at the clinic. Samuel is not tired either.

[sam] Quo imus, Anna? || Where are we going, Anna?
[ana] Ad montem ambulamus. Mons non longe est. || We walk to the hill. The hill is not far.
[sam] Ecce via! Via magna est. || Look, a road! The road is big.
[ana] Bona est. Veni mecum! || It is good. Come with me!

Arbores magnae sunt. In arbore avis est. Avis cantat. || There are big trees. In the tree there is a bird. The bird sings.

[sam] Audi! Avis cantat. || Listen! The bird is singing.
[ana] Parva est, sed bene cantat. Arbor magna est. || It is small, but it sings well. The tree is big.
[sam] Quid est hoc? Estne canis? || What is this? Is it a dog?
[ana] Canis est. Ecce, in via est! Magnus est. || It is a dog. Look, it is on the road! It is big.
[sam] Ille nos videt. Timeo! || It sees us. I am afraid!
[ana] Canis bonus est. Ecce, ad nos venit. || The dog is good. Look, he comes to us.
[sam] Nunc non timeo. Bonus est. || Now I am not afraid. He is good.

Samuel canem timet. Canis ad Annam et Samuelem venit. Anna canem amat. || Samuel is afraid of the dog. The dog comes to Anna and Samuel. Anna loves the dog.

[ana] Nunc ad montem ambulamus. Ecce aqua! || Now we walk to the hill. Look, water!
[sam] Estne aqua ibi? Aquam bibere volo. || Is there water there? I want to drink water.
[ana] Aqua ibi est. Aqua bona est. Veni! || There is water there. The water is good. Come!

Tandem ad montem veniunt. Ibi aquam bibunt. Aqua bona est. || At last they come to the hill. There they drink water. The water is good.
Ibi urbem vident. Urbs non magna est, sed bona est. Domus Marthae parva est. Ecclesia magna est. || There they see the town. The town is not big, but it is good. Martha's house is small. The church is big.

[sam] Ecce urbs! Omnia bona sunt. || Look, the town! All things are good.
[ana] Hodie dies bonus est. Gratias Deo ago. || Today is a good day. I give thanks to God.
[sam] Et ego gratias Deo ago. Et tibi gratias ago, Anna. || And I give thanks to God. And I thank you, Anna.

Tandem ad urbem eunt. Samuel fessus est, sed bene valet. Anna quoque bene valet. || At last they go to the town. Samuel is tired, but he is well. Anna is well too.
