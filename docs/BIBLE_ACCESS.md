No text is copied into GoLearn. This linked edition is separate from the unidentified `bibla-alb` catalogue candidate and the historical Kristoforidhi editions. |

The Albanian standard-language work is kept separate from the historical Kristoforidhi New Testament records in Gheg and Tosk. Those historical varieties are not silently treated as the modern standard edition, and no historical text is bundled by this access registry.

The Scripture page displays the language’s access route directly. A `.txt` or unprotected `.epub` personal copy can be read as an ordinary book; the general importer does not reconstruct Bible verse navigation. It must not be described as an implemented verse-addressed Bible import. Go validates personal files and keeps them on the device. No external Bible text is scraped by the access surface.
