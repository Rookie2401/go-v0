## Vicinus senex
Martha vicinum senem habet. Vicinus bonus est. Senex in domo parva habitat. Nomen ei Petrus est. || Martha has an old neighbour. The neighbour is good. The old man lives in a small house. His name is Peter.
Petrus fessus est, et aquam non habet. Heri quoque aquam non habuit. || Peter is tired, and he does not have water. Yesterday too he did not have water.
Heri Marcus et Samuel ad domum senis venerunt. Senem in porta viderunt. || Yesterday Marcus and Samuel came to the old man's house. They saw the old man at the door.

[nico] Salve, Petre! Quomodo vales? || Hello, Peter! How are you?
[senex] Non bene valeo, Marce. Fessus sum, et aquam non habeo. || I am not well, Marcus. I am tired, and I do not have water.
[sam] Aquam non habes? Ubi est aqua? || You do not have water? Where is the water?
[senex] Aqua longe est. Ego non ambulo. || The water is far away. I do not walk.
[nico] Nos te iuvamus! Aquam tibi damus. || We help you! We give you water.

Marcus et Samuel ad aquam eunt. Aquam habent, et ad domum senis eunt. Tandem senex aquam habet. || Marcus and Samuel go to the water. They have water, and they go to the old man's house. At last the old man has water.

[senex] Gratias vobis ago, pueri! Vos boni estis. || Thank you, boys! You are good.
[sam] Libenter te iuvamus, Petre. Nunc aquam habes. || We gladly help you, Peter. Now you have water.
[senex] Heri in domo fui, et aquam non habui. Hodie vos hic estis. Deus vos amat. || Yesterday I was at home, and I did not have water. Today you are here. God loves you.

Marcus dixit: Cras iterum venimus. Iterum aquam damus. Senex dixit: Gratias Deo ago. || Marcus said: Tomorrow we come again. We give water again. The old man said: I give thanks to God.
Postea Marcus ad domum Marthae venit. Martha Marcum videt. || Afterwards Marcus comes to Martha's house. Martha sees Marcus.

[marta] Ubi fuisti, Marce? || Where were you, Marcus?
[nico] In domo Petri fui, avia. Aquam ei dedi. || I was at Peter's house, grandmother. I gave him water.
[marta] Bene! Petrus vicinus bonus est. Tu quoque puer bonus es. || Good! Peter is a good neighbour. You are a good boy too.
[nico] Samuel mecum fuit. Samuel quoque senem iuvit. || Samuel was with me. Samuel helped the old man too.
[marta] Samuel amicus bonus est. Vos boni estis. Panem vobis do. || Samuel is a good friend. You are good. I give you bread.

Martha panem Marco dat. Postea Samuel venit. Martha dixit: Ecce panis! Panem edite. || Martha gives bread to Marcus. Afterwards Samuel comes. Martha said: Here is bread! Eat the bread.
Marcus et Samuel panem edunt. Fessi sunt, sed bene valent. Multi homines boni in urbe sunt. Vicinus senex quoque bonus est. || They are tired, but they are well. There are many good people in the town. The old neighbour is good too.

Petrus canem habet. Canis bonus est. Canis in via ambulat, et senem amat. || Peter has a dog. The dog is good. The dog walks on the road, and loves the old man.
Hodie Petrus dicit: Caput meum dolet. Anna nutrix est. Anna senem iuvat. || Today Peter says: My head hurts. Anna is a nurse. Anna helps the old man.

[senex] Anna, caput meum dolet. Quid facio? || Anna, my head hurts. What do I do?
[ana] Aquam bibe, Petre! Aqua bona est. Hodie in domo manere bonum est. || Drink water, Peter! Water is good. It is good to stay at home today.
[senex] Gratias tibi ago, Anna. Tu nutrix bona es. Marcus et Samuel quoque boni sunt. || Thank you, Anna. You are a good nurse. Marcus and Samuel are good too.
