# German prose acquisition

The final German library contains 36 works including Bible, 1,860 texts, and 2,523,188 words. The combined source manifest includes 33 Gutenberg editions and the approved Grimm/Hauff Wikisource documents, with official edition URLs, SHA-bound source reviews, and rights evidence. Stable candidate outcomes are in `data/sources/gutenberg/dispositions.json`.

No German entries were refused in the final build. Ambiguous, protected, and missing exact editions remain explicitly listed in the dispositions matrix; no canonical prose was rewritten.
