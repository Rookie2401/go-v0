# Hindi literature bridge

This isolated acquisition check examined two short-story pages already listed in the Hindi literature research matrix. The Hindi Wikisource API identifies its site contribution licence as [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/deed.hi). That licence is recorded for the transcription contribution; it does not by itself clear the underlying scanned publication for worldwide reuse.

Two early stories now pass the isolated bridge gate. *पंच परमेश्वर* and *नमक का दारोगा* are complete proofread story pages under *सप्तसरोज*. The book page identifies the work as *सप्तसरोज* (1917), prints the first-edition preface dated 8 June 1917, and explicitly records PD India and PD US status. The available scan is the 1938 sixteenth edition; it is treated as a reprint of the 1917 work, with no added editorial layer included in the extracted story bodies. The final installed texts contain 4,312 and 3,224 analyzed words respectively. Exact source-file SHA-256 hashes and revision URLs are retained in the source manifest and public provenance. These counts supersede earlier extraction estimates.

The two earlier candidates remain held for a source and rights review: *ईदगाह* is generally dated to first publication in 1933 and is represented here by a 1947 *मानसरोवर १* reprint; *दो बैलों की कथा* is generally dated to first publication in 1931 and is represented here by a 1946 *मानसरोवर २* reprint. The transcription is CC BY-SA 4.0, but those pages do not document whether the reprint text has edition-specific additions or a separately clear worldwide copyright status. The bridge therefore does not treat the reprint year as a new copyright and does not import either pending that evidence. Direct source pages and dispositions remain in `source-research.json`.

Two older alternatives remain useful leads but are not silently substituted: the 1925 *निर्मला* index is marked incomplete, and the 1919 *सेवासदन* index is also incomplete and is a novel rather than a short-story source. Their direct index URLs and dispositions are recorded in the same research file.

Run `npx --no-install vite-node scripts/acquire-hi-literature-bridge.mts` to repeat the isolated acquisition and safety check. It writes only the bridge source directory and `work/hi-literature-check/bridge-check.json`; it does not modify the Hindi course, the public library, or the existing Hindi literature sources.
