## Clavis amissa
Hodie Marcus clavem non habet. Clavis est clavis domus Marthae. || Today Marcus does not have the key. It is the key of Martha's house.
Marcus fessus est. Domum venire vult, sed clavem non habet. || Marcus is tired. He wants to come home, but he does not have the key.

[nico] Ubi est clavis mea? Clavem non habeo! || Where is my key? I do not have the key!
Marcus clavem quaerit. In schola clavem quaerit. Clavis in schola non est. || Marcus looks for the key. He looks for the key at school. The key is not at school.
Marcus in foro clavem quaerit. Clavis in foro non est. || Marcus looks for the key in the market. The key is not in the market.

Samuel Marcum videt. Samuel puerum salutat. || Samuel sees Marcus. Samuel greets the boy.

[sam] Salve, Marce! Quid quaeris? || Hello, Marcus! What are you looking for?
[nico] Clavem quaero. Clavem non habeo. Fessus sum, et domum venire volo. || I am looking for the key. I do not have the key. I am tired, and I want to come home.
[sam] Ego te iuvo! Ubi clavem quaeris? || I will help you! Where are you looking for the key?
[nico] In schola quaero, et in foro quaero. Clavis ibi non est. || I look at school, and I look in the market. The key is not there.
[sam] Estne in ecclesia? Quaerimus in ecclesia! || Is it in the church? Let us look in the church!

Samuel et Marcus in ecclesia clavem quaerunt. Clavis ibi non est. || Samuel and Marcus look for the key in the church. The key is not there.

[nico] Clavis hic non est. Non invenimus! || The key is not here. We do not find it!
[sam] Ubi est Martha? Martha clavem habet? || Where is Martha? Does Martha have the key?
[nico] Martha in domo est. Sed domus clavem non habet. Clavis mea est! || Martha is at home. But the house does not have the key. The key is mine!
[sam] Veni! In domum Marthae venimus. || Come! We come to Martha's house.

Martha in porta est. Marcum et Samuelem videt. || Martha is at the door. She sees Marcus and Samuel.

[marta] Salvete! Quid hic facitis? Quid quaeritis? || Hello! What are you doing here? What are you looking for?
[nico] Clavem quaerimus, avia. Clavem meam non habeo. || We are looking for the key, grandmother. I do not have my key.
[marta] Ecce clavis! Clavis in mensa est, sub pane. Statim clavem video. || Look, the key! The key is on the table, under the bread. I see the key at once.
[nico] Ibi est! Clavem tandem invenio! || It is there! At last I find the key!
[sam] Bene est, Marce! Clavem habes. Tandem clavem invenis. || It is well, Marcus! You have the key. At last you find the key.
[nico] Gratias tibi ago, Samuel. Gratias tibi ago, avia. Tu amicus bonus es. || Thank you, Samuel. Thank you, grandmother. You are a good friend.
[marta] Statim panem edite! Panis sub mensa non est, sed in mensa est. || Eat bread at once! The bread is not under the table, but on the table.

Marcus clavem in manu habet. Ibi panem edit, et lac bibit. Tandem fessus non est. || Marcus has the key in his hand. There he eats bread, and drinks milk. At last he is not tired.
Samuel quoque panem edit. Bene est. Hodie Samuel Marcum iuvat, et Marcus Samuelem iuvat. || Samuel eats bread too. It is well. Today Samuel helps Marcus, and Marcus helps Samuel.
